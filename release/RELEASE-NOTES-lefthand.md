## BioShock VR, left-handed edition

The first release of the left-handed fork of [bioshock-trilogy-vr](https://github.com/VR-Stereo-Hub/bioshock-trilogy-vr) for **BioShock Remastered**.

### Install

1. Download `bioshock-vr-lefthand-<version>.zip` below and unzip it anywhere.
2. Double-click **Install.bat**. It finds the game in your Steam libraries, copies the mod in, backs up anything it replaces, and asks whether you're left-handed.
3. Start your VR runtime (Virtual Desktop with VDXR, Quest Link, or SteamVR) and launch the game from Steam. VR starts by itself.
4. First time only: F10 → **VR camera (M3/M4)** → **Render resolution** → 2560 x 2560 → **Write to Bioshock.ini**, then restart the game.

**Uninstall.bat** removes the mod and puts back anything it replaced.

### What's in it

- **Left-handed mode** with a mirrored viewmodel: a real left hand on the gun, plasmids in your right.
- **Jack's hands on your real hands**, bullets from the barrel you see, and **full arms** from your shoulders.
- **Two-handed grips** on every weapon. Your hand rides the pump, slide and crank, and if you keep holding through a reload you watch Jack do it.
- **A physical EVE hypo** (experimental): draw it from your stomach (or hip), put the needle in your arm, pull the trigger.
- **Recoil and haptics** on every weapon, wrench hits and plasmid casts.
- **Plasmid aim calibration** for your hand, and an aim dot for plasmids.
- **Solid HUD bars**, and the HUD arc artefact around the health gauge is gone.

The README (also `README.txt` in the zip) has the full F10 guide and known limits.
