BioShock VR - left-handed edition
=================================

1. Double-click Install.bat.
   It finds BioShock Remastered in your Steam libraries, copies the mod in,
   backs up anything it replaces, and asks whether you're left-handed.

2. Start your VR runtime (Virtual Desktop with VDXR, Quest Link, or SteamVR),
   then launch BioShock Remastered from Steam. VR starts by itself.

3. First time only: press F10 in game -> "VR camera (M3/M4)" ->
   "Render resolution", pick 2560 x 2560, press "Write to Bioshock.ini" and
   restart the game. A square image is sharper and faster in a headset.

README.txt explains every F10 setting a player needs.
TROUBLESHOOTING.txt covers "no VR / flat screen" problems.
Uninstall.bat removes the mod and puts back anything it replaced.

Your settings and log live in %LOCALAPPDATA%\BioshockVR\
